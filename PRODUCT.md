# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Daily RSS/Atom readers on desktop and mobile who value privacy, focus, offline access, and control over algorithmic discovery. EN/TR/ES.

## Product Purpose

Local-first feed reading: users pick sources, folders, filters, and notification behavior, then find, read, save, and resume articles without attention traps. No accounts, tracking, algorithmic timeline, or ads.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, personal. Ice-cream identity adds warmth; functionality stays serious. Friendly utility, never noisy.

## Anti-references

Dense enterprise-reader chrome; engagement loops; telemetry/upsell surfaces; visually noisy multi-border dashboards.

## Design Principles

- Content leads; decoration supports, never competes.
- Clear hierarchy over chrome density.
- Wide layouts cap content width; phone layouts stay compact.
- Local-first promise shows in the UI: no account/tracking surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

48dp targets, contrast, reduced motion respected, responsive widths, EN/TR/ES localization.
